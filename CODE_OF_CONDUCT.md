# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Beta-Stock a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, evidence-based feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when a mistake causes harm;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people affected by the software;
- discussing investing knowledge without pressuring anyone to disclose finances, identity, location, or personal data; and
- disclosing relevant conflicts of interest when discussing brokers, data vendors, securities, or financial products.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- manipulating, shaming, or pressuring someone about financial decisions;
- knowingly posting credentials, private account data, licensed datasets, or deceptive market claims; and
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

These standards apply in every project space and when a person officially represents Beta-Stock in a public space.

## Private reporting

Do not place a sensitive conduct report in a public issue or discussion. Use the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and prefix the private report title with `CONDUCT:`. This uses GitHub's private maintainer conversation solely as a confidential intake channel; a conduct report does not need to describe a software vulnerability.

Include the relevant links or dates, a factual description, and any immediate safety concern. Omit credentials, unrelated personal data, and licensed content. A maintainer named in the report will not handle that report. If the repository channel is unavailable or the concern involves all maintainers, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers will acknowledge a report as soon as practical, preserve confidentiality to the extent possible, avoid retaliation, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when doing so is safe and lawful.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use these impact guidelines when deciding consequences:

### 1. Correction

For inappropriate language or other behavior judged unprofessional: a private written warning explaining the violation and, when relevant, a request for a public correction or apology.

### 2. Warning

For a single incident or a series of violations: a warning with conditions on continued participation. During the stated period, the person may not interact with the people involved, including unsolicited contact outside project spaces.

### 3. Temporary ban

For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior: a time-limited ban from project interaction or public representation. Violating the terms may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

For a pattern of violations, harassment, aggression toward individuals, or disparagement based on a protected characteristic: permanent removal from project participation.
